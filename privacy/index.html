YouTube Comment Kit

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

1. Single Purpose & Data Processing

YouTube Comment Kit has a single purpose: enhancing your YouTube experience with comment search, timed comments, and comment history viewing.

  • YouTube Comments & History: All comment search indexing and history processing occur 100% locally on your device. No video IDs, comment text, search keywords, or browsing history are ever sent to our servers.
  • Pro License Verification: If you activate a Pro plan, your license key is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to our license proxy endpoint (https://verify-and-sign-proxy.nadirabbas.workers.dev/verify-and-sign) to validate your active subscription. The resulting verification token is stored locally in your browser.

2. Limited Use Policy Compliance

In full compliance with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies:

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer user data to any third party.
  • We do not use user data for advertising, marketing, tracking, or user profiling.
  • We only access data strictly necessary to operate disclosed extension features.

3. Data Storage & User Control

All extension preferences, local comment caches, and license tokens are stored locally on your machine using standard browser storage. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the extension.
